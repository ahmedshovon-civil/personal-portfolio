01 / Profile

About me

An academic foundation in civil engineering, with growing interests in research, material behaviour, and structural systems.

My undergraduate studies have provided a foundation in structural mechanics, reinforced concrete design, soil mechanics, foundation engineering, and construction technology.

My academic interests bring together structural performance and the development of construction materials. I am interested in recycled concrete materials, cement-based composites, and emerging construction methods such as 3D-printed concrete.

I aim to strengthen my research skills and pursue advanced study in structural engineering and sustainable construction materials.
